10 • Detailed business plan

The model, organised for execution.

Positioning

Managed Living sits between informal flat-sharing, premium co-living and serviced apartments. The differentiation is low density, stronger home quality, professional operations and neighbourhood clustering. It should not be marketed as a cheaper PG.

Core promise: great house + good location + vetted household + housekeeping + linen + Wi-Fi + maintenance + security + flexible tenure + neighbourhood services.

11 • Risk & diligence

Do not scale before the legal classification and service model are clean.

Regulatory classification

Room-by-room managed homes may fall under PG, lodging, tenancy or other local rules depending on structure. Obtain Bangalore legal/tax advice before launch.

Fire, CCTV & safety

If treated as a PG/accommodation establishment, current local requirements can include CCTV, occupancy norms, fire safety and operating standards.

Food compliance

If food is cooked in-house rather than supplied by a compliant partner, FSSAI and kitchen rules may apply.

Vacancy

Short-stay fill, corporate demand and flexible room formats mitigate vacancy without changing the core long-stay model.

Service complexity

Keep food, laundry, gym and mobility partner-led initially.

Resident conflict

Internal matching, house rules and transfers between nearby homes get stronger with cluster density.

The thesis

The scalable asset is the operating network, not the furniture.

Owner relationships + resident demand + neighbourhood density + operating systems + trust can become the moat.

Start concentrated.
